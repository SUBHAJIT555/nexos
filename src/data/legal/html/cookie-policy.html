Cookie Policy of NexEco AI

This cookie policy governs how NexEco AI ("NexEco AI", "we", "our" or "us") uses cookies on the NexEco AI website (our "Site").

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small (temporary) text files we transfer to your device, that enable the collection of information from your device ("Cookies"). Please refer to our Privacy Policy for information about how we process personal data collected on our Site.

Why does NexEco AI use Cookies?

NexEco AI uses Cookies on the Site to operate the Site and measure website audience. We use the following categories of Cookies:

  • Technical and Functional Cookies: These cookies are essential for our Site’s operation and cannot be disabled. They are typically set only in response to actions taken by you that constitute a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms. You can configure your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site may not work properly as a result.

  • Analytical Cookies: These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our Site. They help us understand which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the Site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous. If you do not allow these cookies, we will not know when you have visited our Site and will not be able to monitor its performance.

  • Tracking Cookies: These cookies are used to collect data regarding your browsing behavior across different websites. They help in creating user profiles by tracking the pages you visit, the links you click, and the frequency of your returns to our Site. This information is used for targeted advertising and personalizing your online experience.

How can I make choices about Cookies and my privacy?

The Technical and Functional Cookies are set by default and cannot be changes, as changing these settings would prevent our Site from functioning properly.

When you withhold your consent, this does not automatically delete any Cookies already stored on your device from previous consents. You will need to delete these Cookies manually by following the instructions on your browser (these are usually located within the "Help" or "Preference" menu).
